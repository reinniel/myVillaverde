# Three-detent map sheet fix

The map sheet now uses three real heights instead of moving a giant panel off-screen:

1. Compact: the grabber, title, and subtitle are fully visible.
2. Preview/Browse: roughly half-screen and independently scrollable.
3. Full: near-full-screen and independently scrollable.

Marker taps open the middle place preview. The existing myVillaverde category and place-card design is retained; only the sheet mechanism was changed.
